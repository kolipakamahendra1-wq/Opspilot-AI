# Guide: Shipping Statuses

- IN_TRANSIT: with the carrier and within the ETA. No action needed; share the ETA with the customer.
- DELAYED: carrier reported a delay. See the carrier delay runbook.
- ADDRESS_ISSUE: carrier cannot deliver to the address. See the address runbook.
- DELIVERED: carrier confirmed delivery.

Carriers: BlueDart, DHL, FedEx, UPS. Where is my package? Check the shipping status and ETA first.
